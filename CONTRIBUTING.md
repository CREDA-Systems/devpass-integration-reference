# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in the DevPass® Integration Reference repository.

This repository is maintained by CREDA Systems as a limited public integration surface for interoperability, developer tooling, credential portability, and ecosystem-specific reference work.

## Contribution Scope

Contributions may be appropriate where they improve:

* public integration documentation;
* developer-record schemas;
* attestation schemas;
* verification-result schemas;
* fictional example payloads;
* validation utilities;
* interoperability guidance;
* public integrations; or
* developer usability.

Contributions should remain within the scope of the public repository.

## Do Not Submit

Please do not submit:

* real personal information;
* biometric data;
* production identity records;
* confidential employment or education records;
* proprietary DevPass® verification logic;
* identity-binding methods;
* scoring or ranking logic;
* fraud-detection methods;
* live-defense procedures;
* clearance decisioning;
* production credentials or secrets;
* unpublished patent-sensitive material;
* confidential customer or partner information;
* proprietary third-party code;
* material subject to nondisclosure obligations; or
* content you do not have the right to publish.

If you are unsure whether material is appropriate for public contribution, do not submit it through a public issue or pull request.

## VTI Standards and Certification

This repository is not a standards-development or certification venue for VTI Foundation Inc.

Issues and pull requests do not amend, interpret, supersede, or establish requirements for any VTI Foundation Inc. standard, certification program, or conformity-assessment process.

## Issues

Issues may be used to report:

* documentation errors;
* schema inconsistencies;
* validation problems;
* interoperability questions;
* defects in publicly released integration code; or
* other matters directly related to repository contents.

Do not use public issues to report security vulnerabilities or disclose sensitive information.

See `SECURITY.md` for security reporting guidance.

## Pull Requests

Pull requests should:

* address a clearly defined issue or improvement;
* remain narrowly scoped;
* avoid unrelated structural changes;
* include documentation where behavior changes;
* include tests where applicable;
* use fictional or synthetic data only; and
* accurately describe what has and has not been implemented.

Experimental or incomplete work should not be described as production-ready.

Do not submit material subject to incompatible licensing terms.

* patent rights outside the scope, if any, of the applicable repository license; or

## Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to engage professionally, focus on technical substance, and avoid abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or disruptive behavior.

CREDA Systems reserves the right to moderate participation in order to maintain a constructive technical environment.

## Questions

Questions about contribution scope may be raised through an appropriate repository issue unless they involve confidential, security-sensitive, or unpublished material.

For matters involving licensing, trademarks, proprietary verification technology, privacy, or other intellectual-property issues, use official CREDA Systems contact channels.
